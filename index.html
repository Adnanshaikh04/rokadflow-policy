Privacy Policy for Rokad Flow

Effective Date: 2026

1. Introduction

Rokad Flow is a personal finance management application designed to help users track income, expenses, and financial activities.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled.

2. Data Collection

Rokad Flow is an offline-first application.

All financial data including expenses, income, categories, and notes is stored locally on your device only.

We do not collect, store, or transmit your financial data to our servers.

3. Google Drive Backup

The app provides an optional backup feature using Google Drive.

  • Users can sign in with their Google account
  • Backup is stored in appDataFolder (private folder)

We do not access or store your data on our servers.

4. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads.

AdMob may collect device information, advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data.

5. File Access

The app allows users to select backup files manually. No files are accessed without user action.

6. Permissions

Storage: for backup and restore

Internet: for Google Drive and ads

7. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

8. Data Security

Your data is stored locally or in your Google Drive account.

9. User Control

You can delete your data anytime by uninstalling the app or removing backups.

10. Children’s Privacy

Not intended for children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this policy anytime.
